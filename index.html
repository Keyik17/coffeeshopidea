Good questions, straight answers.

How does installation work?
We handle the whole setup. Your guest Wi-Fi is redirected to the branded login page. It's usually a quick, remote configuration. There's nothing for you to build or maintain, and no disruption to your existing internet.
Do customers actually agree to be contacted?
Yes. Every customer opts in on the login page before they connect, and the page clearly states they'll receive occasional texts. Every message includes a one-tap way to opt out (reply STOP), so your list stays consent-based and compliant.
Does it work with my current Wi-Fi?
In almost all cases, yes. The system is designed to sit on top of your existing guest network and standard router setups. On a quick call we'll confirm compatibility before you commit to anything.
How many texts will you send my customers?
Between 4 and 8 per month: enough to stay top of mind, not so many that people tune out. We optimize the timing and offers so each message earns its place, and we never over-send.
Can I cancel? Is there a contract?
No contract. The plan is month to month and you can cancel anytime. We'd rather keep you because it's working than lock you into a term.
How does the free trial work?
Your first 30 days are free. We place a card on file when you start, but you're not charged during the trial. After 30 days, your first paid month is backed by our money-back guarantee.
